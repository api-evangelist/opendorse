Opendorse One™ Athletes Debut as Initial Wave of Creator Storefronts, Connecting College Sports’ Most Influential Athletes to Commerce at Scale (Lincoln, Neb. and San Francisco, Calif. – June 19, 2026) – Opendorse, the technology, data, and service platform powering the Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) industry, today announced a strategic partnership with Shopsense AI, the content-to-commerce pioneer, to launch AI-powered shoppable storefronts across the Opendorse Commerce Media Network.
