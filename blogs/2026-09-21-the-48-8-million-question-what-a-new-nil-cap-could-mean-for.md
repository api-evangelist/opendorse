Summer is usually the quiet season in college sports. Not this one. Between June and August, Congress worked on a bill that could more than double the athlete compensation cap, the NCAA rewrote the rules on athlete eligibility, small-college conferences started building their own NIL frameworks, and the compliance thresholds that govern how brand deals get reviewed changed outright.
