(Modesto, Calif. and Lincoln, Neb. – August 24, 2026) – Modesto Junior College Athletics has signed a three-year partnership with Opendorse, the leading Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) technology, data, and service provider, giving Pirates student-athletes access to Opendorse’s full platform for building their brands and managing opportunities.
