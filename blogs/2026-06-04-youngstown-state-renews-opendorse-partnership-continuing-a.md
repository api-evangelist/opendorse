(Youngstown, Ohio and Lincoln, Neb. – June 4, 2026) – Youngstown State University Athletics has renewed its partnership with Opendorse, the leading NIL technology, data, and service provider. The renewal builds on a relationship that began in 2021, when Youngstown State became one of the early Division I programs to invest in an NIL solution for its student-athletes.
