3D-printed footwear brand puts real college athletes in its shoes through a low-risk, product-based approach as it builds a broader NIL strategy (Lincoln, Neb. – October 2, 2026) – Syntilay has announced a new partnership with Opendorse, the leading Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) technology, data, and service provider, to scale its entry into college athlete marketing. Built around 3D-printed footwear technology, Syntilay will use the partnership to put college athletes in its shoes as it grows its presence in the NIL space.
